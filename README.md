# Who Stole the Sun? · Qui a volé le Soleil ? · ¿Quién robó el Sol?

A pocket detective notebook. Six small towns, six miniature suns gone missing.
Read the exhibits, cross-check the timelines, mark your board — then name the
**culprit**, the **scene of the theft** and the **decisive exhibit** before the judge.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any browser — that is the whole game (one
self-contained file, no network, no install). English by default, French and
Spanish included (EN / FR / ES selector).

## The game

- **6 hand-written cases** of rising difficulty (1 to 5 suns), each with
  5 suspects, 3–4 places and a short timeline; case 6 closes the story.
- **Exhibits** (witness statements, records, found objects, leads) are freely
  readable at any time.
- **Hypothesis board**: mark every suspect and place as *present*, *possible*
  or *absent*. Your notes are yours — the game never checks them.
- **Fair-play deduction**: every case has exactly one solution. A statement
  about oneself never suffices; exclusions always come from verifiable facts
  (a register, a third party, a physical trace). No hidden rule to guess.
- **Wrong accusations** get a useful reply from the judge (it cites the exhibit
  that contradicts you) but never the answer.
- **Progressive hints** (3 per case) for stuck players — they dim your stars.
- **Full reasoning** is revealed on victory, step by step, with clickable
  references back to the exhibits, plus a gentle epilogue.
- Touch-friendly and responsive; keyboard and screen-reader basics (ARIA tabs,
  pressed states); no essential clue depends on colour alone.

## Language of publication

The game itself ships **EN (default) / FR / ES**, fully localised — cases,
clues, judge, hints and explanations. No AI is called at runtime.

## Development

Built automatically with Z-Code (GLM). Concept: Cédric Bernadac. The six cases
were hand-written and formally validated: every case's clue set is checked to
exclude all-but-one culprit and all-but-one scene (brute-force uniqueness test),
and the decisive exhibit is required to be *necessary* (it eliminates at least
one suspect no other exhibit eliminates). The validation suite runs against the
delivered `index.html` itself.
